---
title: Labor
order: 4
lead: "In my work, I distinguish labor from work."
---

Work is the activities to create an output; it has an end. Labor, in contrast, is the ongoing process in creating that output; the conditions within which workers create the outputs. In distinguishing labor from work, my research adopts a critical perspective on how management mediates workers’ experiences in their jobs. Labor allows attention to the political and economic implications in configurations of work, and my research explores how these may be changed toward different ends.
