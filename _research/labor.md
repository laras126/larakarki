title: Labor Conditions
order: 4
---

Labor is the ongoing processes that govern the conditions within which work happes. In distinguishing labor from work, my research adopts a critical perspective on how management mediates workers’ experiences in their jobs. My research examines what are the political and economic implications in configurations of work, and asks how they can be changed toward different ends.
