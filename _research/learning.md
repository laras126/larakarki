order: 3

Work-based learning occurs through participation -- often beginning with low-stakes tasks that require little prior knowledge. In these on-the-job learning experiences, what workers learn may be so tightly situated in the work context their knowledge and learning isn't recognized. In this research area, I study how workers are learning computing through work, and it can be recognized.
